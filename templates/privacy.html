Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects only the data necessary to provide you with a personal expense tracking service. We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Email address, password (securely hashed), and name when you create an account.
  • Expense data: Transaction amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and any notes you add.
  • Usage data: Basic interaction data such as login timestamps and feature usage to improve the Service.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as government IDs, financial account numbers, or precise geolocation data.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Service. Specifically:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To store, display, and organize your expense entries.
  • To generate spending summaries, category breakdowns, and reports for your personal use.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To communicate important updates about the Service (e.g., security notices, policy changes).

We do not use your expense data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing the expense tracking functionality.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard protections:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Database access is restricted to the application server only.
  • Regular backups are performed to prevent data loss.

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share your data with any third parties except as described below:

  • Hosting provider: Our cloud hosting provider (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud) processes data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal obligations: We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: View all data associated with your account within the application.
  • Correction: Update or correct your expense entries at any time.
  • Deletion: Delete individual expenses or your entire account.
  • Export: Request a copy of your data in a portable format (coming soon).

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the Service (e.g., a banner or notification) at least 30 days before the new policy takes effect. Your continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
